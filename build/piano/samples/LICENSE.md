These 30 recordings are from the Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm (a Yamaha C5), licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Source: https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html (SalamanderGrandPianoV3+20161209_44khz16bit).

Changed for this app: one strength (v10 of 16) of each note was taken, cut to start 2 ms before the hammer and to
last 4.5 s (A0) to 2 s (C8) with the last 0.4 s faded out, brought to one loudness, and encoded as stereo MP3
(LAME -V5). Their tuning is measured and corrected when played (../samples.json). scripts/piano-samples.ts does all
of it.
